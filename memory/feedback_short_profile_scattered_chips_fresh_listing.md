**Rule:** The desk's short book gains a positive screen, not just vetoes. SHORTABLE profile: (a) fresh Korean-exchange (Upbit/Bithumb) listing pump, (b) Binance/major spot market EXISTS, (c) chips scattered — big unlocks, distributed float, no team mega-safe control. In that structure the operator cannot afford to hold price at highs (spot-side control cost is prohibitive), so the pump is a short-squeeze + hot-money spike that fades (KAT: Upbit listing, high pre-pump short ratio squeezed, scattered spot chips → dealer "can't drag it out with you at high levels", 2048532824157036657; OPG same skeleton 2057735046849778103). Second shortable class: late-stage BIG-DISTRIBUTION spot names with months of turnover behind them (pippin/river class) — shortable on pullbacks for a month+, low risk (2043669356849332725). INVERSE GATE: a high control-rate name that finished distributing can re-accumulate at lows and run a ROUND 2 — control rate stays high through the dump, so "round 1 ended" is NOT a short thesis (2044341603675017310).

**Why:** §6 currently defines shorts by phase signatures (Cat B top, Stage 4-5, blowoff). This adds the CHIP-STRUCTURE precondition that decides whether ANY short works: scattered chips = no one is paid to defend the price = fades stick; controlled chips = every breakdown is potentially the operator's wash for round 2. It's the same chips-first logic as §0.6 #1 applied to the short side, from the analyst whose LAB/RAVE/RIVER reads built §4. Even on the shortable profile he still didn't short KAT mid-squeeze — liquidity good, squeeze live — the profile times the STRUCTURE, phase signatures still time the ENTRY.

**How to apply:**
- Add to any Stage 4-5 / distribution-top short workup: chip-structure check (holder concentration, unlock overhang, fresh KR listing flag, spot venue map). Scattered = short candidate; concentrated = require full §6 Stage 4-5 confluence AND accept round-2 risk.
- Screener candidate: KR-listing calendar + Binance-spot flag + holder-concentration + unlock data (`unlocks` capability exists) → "fadeable cohort" list, surfacing shorts that are NOT operator-defended.
- Retail shorts crowding a fresh listing that never got shaken = fuel for the next bot leg (OPG) — wait for the crowd's fuel to burn before fading.
- Trapped-short management on a squeeze name: lock/hedge + wait for the top structure (top-ST), don't panic-cover (2047663621099139306).
