# Same-signature scouts = one regime bet (the 2026-07-08 double stop)

**What happened:** the desk carried two scout longs — BASED (post-blowoff spring) and SLX
(trap-formation squeeze) — both built on the same signature family: held low + negative funding +
OI-loading shorts. Overnight 07-08, LAB (the complex's biggest chronic squeezer) went terminal:
−56% to 6.2 on $1.7B volume, funding pinned ~−10%/4h-equivalent. The entire microcap squeeze
complex re-priced simultaneously (BIRB −14.5%, VELVET −19%, TAIKO −9.5%, TLM −19.5%) and **both
longs stopped within the same window**, −1R each.

**The tell that it was regime, not thesis:** neither counterparty kill-switch fired — SLX's 0x8e4b
stayed frozen through the break; BASED's fleet was still 9/9 holding with the funder net-in at the
lows. The operators didn't sell; the *shorts stopped being squeezable* everywhere at once because
LAB proved that a deep-neg squeezer can cascade instead of squeeze, and every trapped short on the
tape got paid and emboldened.

**Why (mechanics):** "deep-neg + OI-rising + held low" is not independent evidence across names in
the same regime — the shorts pressing all these books are substantially the same flow/psychology,
and their conviction is set by the flagship name. When the flagship breaks, the discriminator
(trapped shorts must capitulate) flips sign complex-wide in one session.

**How to apply:**
1. **Count same-signature positions as one bet.** Two squeeze-fuel longs ≈ 1.5–2R exposure to a
   single regime event, whatever the tickers. Cap combined same-signature risk the way §7 caps
   per-operator heat (~same 6% spirit; at scout tier: max ~1R combined, not 1R each).
2. **The complex's biggest live squeezer is the regime canary.** Before/while holding any
   squeeze-fuel long, know the flagship's state (then: LAB). Flagship terminal-cascading = tighten
   or exit ALL same-signature longs — don't wait for each name's own level.
3. Position-level discipline WORKED: −1R each, stops beyond true wicks fired before oracle liqs,
   apparatus facts (still-holding fleets) survive as reload watches. The gap was portfolio-level
   correlation, not trade construction.
