When a Cat A is ripping to fresh ATHs, do NOT read "mega-safes dormant / no on-chain distribution" as a bullish, squeeze-has-fuel, don't-short signal. It only proves the *on-chain* exit is cold — and OTC block deals settle desk-to-desk, **never touching a hot wallet** (Section 9 Rule 7). A coin up huge to fresh ATHs with large locked insider supply is the *prototypical* OTC-exit setup; the engineered squeeze is the very demand/attention event that manufactures the liquidity for the operator to unload OTC. "Team not selling on-chain" may be *because* they're selling off-chain.

**Why (LAB 2026-06-01):** in the LAB deep-dive I scored "all 10 WITHDRAW-FRESH wallets nonce=0 + 5 mega-safes dormant" as a strong LONG-supporting / squeeze-has-room signal. User pushed: "have you taken in the possibility of OTC deals?" — correct, I'd overweighted it. LAB was +90%/24h, ~12,000%/88d, fresh $16 ATH, ~$15B locked supply = textbook OTC-exit conditions.

**How to apply:**
1. On a parabola, downgrade "on-chain clean" to **neutral / necessary-but-not-sufficient**, never bullish. You cannot see whether the operator is exiting OTC.
2. Rule 7's classic OTC tell (price *bleeding* while on-chain pristine) won't fire when price is *ripping* — absence of that tell is NOT evidence they're holding; it just means the OTC exit (if any) is happening under cover of strength.
3. **Don't gate a blowoff-top short on an on-chain fire** (WITHDRAW-FRESH / safe outbound). If they exit OTC those wallets may never move and the top comes anyway. Make the **perp/price tells primary** (funding flip toward flat/positive + breakdown-that-holds); on-chain fire = bonus, not required.
4.
