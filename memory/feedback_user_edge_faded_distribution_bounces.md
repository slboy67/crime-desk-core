**The user's stated primary edge (lean the desk toward it):** Short the **bounces of already-played-out crime coins that are still distributing post-dump, after attention has drifted away.** Why it's the high-win sweet spot:
- **Attention has faded** → fewer retail eyes, no live squeeze machine, no operator actively defending a bid. The dangerous active-AMM-pump dynamics (deep-neg squeeze, §5 veto, getting farmed) are gone.
- **Distribution reads clean** → on a played-out name the operator is methodically exiting the remainder; the on-chain signal is low-noise and easy to read vs. a hot pump where MM churn obscures everything.
- You **fade the dead-cat bounces** in the established post-dump downtrend — sell strength into the operator's exit.

Examples this session that fit the mold: ESPORTS (−94% off ATH, distributing, faded — the held short), SKYAI (−80% off ATH, dumping to Bitget, faded), and the EDEN scalp short. The *opposite* (hard/avoid): hot active pumps — LAB, OPN, EDEN-while-squeezing — deep-neg, squeeze risk, vetoed.

**How to apply (orchestrator):**
- **Prioritize surfacing** names that fit: heavily off-ATH (played out) + on-chain distribution active (verify the wallets — [[feedback_cat_a_verify_onchain_trigger_not_triage_notrade]]) + a recent bounce to fade + attention faded (declining volume/social) + funding **flat-or-positive, NOT deep-neg** (deep-neg = still an active squeeze, wrong phase). This is sub-edge #2 in CLAUDE.md §1 ("SHORT at distribution top, fades as attention dies"), sharpened to *post-peak, attention-gone* names — and it applies to faded Cat A, not just Cat B.
- **Deprioritize** the hot active pumps for shorting (squeeze fuel, veto, exit-liquidity risk). Surface them as longs/blowoff-watches only.
- Tell the user where the played-out-distributing bounces are; that's the factory. A `screener`/scan "faded-distribution" mode (off-ATH + distributing + low-attention) would serve this directly — candidate desk capability.
