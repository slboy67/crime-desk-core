**Rule:** On operator-controlled names, position exits key off the OPERATOR'S floating profit, not your target. The reservoir cycle (STO worked example 2039627137901355470): squeeze up → main force clears its own floating long profit (deleverage leg, price drops, also a shakeout) → re-squeeze → repeat until squeeze fuel exhausts → one needle down through every technical level → re-accumulate. Consequences: (1) when main-force longs carry large floating profit, an instant "一根针" far below the obvious stops is ALWAYS live — betting they won't pull it has negative EV over time (2042086579561492496); (2) bank floating profit into consolidation in a bear-structure market — "don't eat the last wave; eat the fish body" (2041919231160414524, 2044342091208425542); (3) no overnight crime-coin perp holds (2040461187130036508); (4) breakeven stop after the first leg, then let it run — the re-entry problem ("if I TP here, where do I get back in?") argues against premature full exits during a live squeeze (2044441442115064173).

**Why:** The desk's stopped trades and the SKYAI −30% share a shape: position held into a phase where the operator's book, not the market, decides the print. §0.6 sizes to exit liquidity; this adds WHEN the exit has to be taken: the operator's floating profit is the fuse on your position. It also refines the time_stop concept — the real time stop is the operator's deleveraging schedule (post-squeeze consolidation), not a calendar duration.

**How to apply:**
- Long theses on Cat A squeezes: TP plan must include a "deleveraging leg" assumption — pre-commit to banking a tranche at squeeze-stall (OI stops making highs / liq clusters above thin out), not at a price target.
- Add to thesis blocks a `operator_profit` note where estimable (entry zone of main-force OI vs current price): wide floating profit = needle risk elevated, tighten management.
- Overnight rule: crime-coin perp positions get closed or breakeven-stopped before off-desk hours; the 2AM dump into sleeping longs is a named operator tactic ($H, 2064107028445069395).
- Spot-first on conviction longs — perp positions get washed off before the real leg; spot is the only instrument that survives the washes (2050222620172554534).
